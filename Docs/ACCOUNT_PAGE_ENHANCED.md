## 🎨 Design System

### **Colors Used:**
- Primary: `var(--primary)` - Main brand color
- Primary Light: `var(--primary-light)` - Accents
- Primary XLight: `var(--primary-xlight)` - Backgrounds
- Gray scale: `gray-50` to `gray-900` - Text and backgrounds
- Success: Green - Positive states
- Error: Red - Negative states
- Info: Blue - Informational

### **Typography:**
- Headings: Bold, large sizes
- Body: Regular weight, readable sizes
- Labels: Semibold, smaller sizes
- Badges: Bold, small sizes

### **Spacing:**
- Small: 0.5rem - 1rem
- Medium: 1.5rem - 2rem
- Large: 2.5rem - 3rem
- Consistent throughout

### **Shadows:**
- `shadow-sm` - Cards and containers
- `shadow-md` - Hover states
- `shadow-lg` - Avatars and highlights

### **Border Radius:**
- `rounded-lg` - Buttons and inputs
- `rounded-xl` - Cards and containers
- `rounded-full` - Avatars and badges
